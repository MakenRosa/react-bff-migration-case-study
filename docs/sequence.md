# Conceptual modernization checklist

This is a general engineering checklist, not an internal delivery plan or a record of a company's rollout.

1. Identify the responsibilities of a screen and the information it needs.
2. Define the contract between frontend and API.
3. Implement and validate the relevant components and integration behavior.
4. Check expected behavior before replacing an existing flow.
5. Review the result and maintain the contract as requirements evolve.

No schedules, rollout stages, internal measurements or business requirements are disclosed.
